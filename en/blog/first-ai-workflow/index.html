How to pick your first AI workflow

Choose a narrow, measurable task before connecting an AI assistant to the rest of the business.

OminiCorp Editorial

Start with a task that happens often, has a clear outcome, and can tolerate an imperfect first draft. Summarising an internal meeting or organising public feedback can be better starting points than a task with legal or financial consequences.

Define what success means

Pick real examples and decide what a reviewer must check. Measure time saved after review, not just how quickly the model returns an answer.

Draw a data boundary

Decide what may be sent to the provider before the pilot. Keep sensitive data out of the workflow unless you have a clear reason and approval.

Make review part of the workflow

Make it easy to compare the result with the source. Track common errors and revisit them before expanding the pilot.
